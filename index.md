#### Information Collection and Use
For a better experience while using our Service, I may require you to provide us with certain information.

**Microphone and Speaker Data (Tuner and Ear Training)**
2Jam requires access to your device's microphone to provide its core functionalities, such as the instrument tuner and interactive ear training exercises. The audio data captured by the microphone is processed strictly in real-time on your device to detect pitch and frequency. Additionally, the app uses your device's speakers to play reference tones, exercises, and metronome beats. **We do not record, store, transmit, or share any audio data, voice recordings, or sounds captured by your microphone or played through your speakers to any external servers.**

**User Progress and Local Data**
To enhance your learning experience, 2Jam stores certain data locally on your device, such as your daily streaks, ear training progress, and fretboard training statistics. This information is kept entirely on your device and is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

**In-App Purchases**
2Jam offers premium features and subscriptions through in-app purchases. All payment transactions are securely processed and handled directly by Apple's App Store via StoreKit. We do not collect, process, or have access to your credit card details or billing information. 

**Advertising and Tracking**
Version 1.1.6 and later of 2Jam contains no advertising and does not track you. It does not use Google AdMob, does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not collect advertising identifiers.

*Earlier versions (1.1.5 and earlier):* the free version displayed advertisements through **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and usage data to provide personalized and non-personalized ads, and those versions asked for your permission under App Tracking Transparency on iOS 14.5 and later. You can manage that permission at any time in your device's Settings.
